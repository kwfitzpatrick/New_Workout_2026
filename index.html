Program guide

Weeks 1–3 · Foundation: Keep 2–4 reps in reserve, focus on technique, and start with fewer sets if coming back from a break.

Week 4 · Easier week: Reduce sets ~30–40%; maintain easy, controlled movement.

Weeks 5–7 · Build: Gradually add reps or load while maintaining control.

Week 8 · Easier week: Reduce sets ~30–40% again.

Weeks 9–11 · Progress: Heavier main lifts only if pain-free; no maximal-effort reps.

Week 12 · Consolidate: Repeat strong, controlled performances; check progress and plan the next block.

With spinal stenosis, persistent back pain, and a surgically fused ankle, have a clinician or physical therapist review the program before beginning. Avoid forcing ankle motion, spinal loading that triggers symptoms, or any movement that causes radiating pain, numbness, or weakness. Pick the easier variation where appropriate. This is general fitness guidance, not individualized medical care.
